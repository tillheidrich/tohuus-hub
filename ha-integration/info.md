## ToHuus Hub

Your self-hosted household – stock, shopping list, meal plan, care tasks, party orders – as a native Home Assistant integration.

- **To-do list** for the shopping list, two-way and Assist-ready ("add milk to the shopping list")
- **Calendars** for the meal plan and recurring care tasks
- **Sensors**: tonight's meal with photo, running low, run out, best before soon, eat first, open orders, care due, party, kid wishes
- **Actions**: add to list, consume, used up, add stock, plan a meal, order from the sofa, answer kid wishes, complete care tasks
- **Events** on the bus (`tohuus_hub_event`) and an event entity – new order, low stock, care due …
- **Kitchen display** images, **buttons** for receipt inbox, fill the week and backup
- **Two-way bridge**: keeps Bring!, the built-in shopping list, Mealie or a family calendar in step with ToHuus Hub, knows who is home, sends notifications through the companion app, turns NFC tag scans into quick actions
- Live updates over server-sent events, ready-made dashboards (core cards and Mushroom)

Set up: create a "Home Assistant" access in ToHuus Hub (Settings › Access & AI), then add the integration and paste address and token.
