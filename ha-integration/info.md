## ToHuus Hub

ToHuus remembers what is missing at home. This integration brings it into Home Assistant – with **ToHuus Cloud** or your **own ToHuus Hub**.

- **To-do list** for the shopping list, two-way and Assist-ready ("add milk to the shopping list")
- **Calendar** for the week plan
- **Sensors**: due to buy (purchase rhythm), shopping list, spent this month / week, tonight's meal, running low, eat first
- **Notifications** from ToHuus to your notify services (phone, TV, speaker)
- **Who leads** per topic when you also use Bring!, the built-in shopping list, Mealie or a family calendar
- **Actions** and **events** for automations, ready-made dashboards
- Live updates; Home Assistant only connects out, nothing at home has to be reachable from the internet

Set up: in ToHuus tap *Settings › Home Assistant › Connect Home Assistant*, then add the integration here, choose ToHuus Cloud or Own server and enter the code.
