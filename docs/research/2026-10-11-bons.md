# Receipts from German supermarkets and drugstores – what they look like (2026-10-11)

Research for the "quality" area: make receipt recognition robust for the big German chains, learn from
household corrections, and let people contribute anonymised receipts without handing over private data.

**No real receipts or copyrighted images were committed.** The fixtures in `test/fixtures/receipts/quality/`
are synthetic: written by hand to mimic the line structures described below (names, addresses and numbers are
made up – "Musterstraße", "99999 Musterstadt", fake card numbers). Real receipts used for checking the
anonymisation stay in the git-ignored `test/fixtures/private/`.

## How digital receipts reach a household

| Chain | Digital receipt | Format a household can get at | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| REWE | eBon in the REWE app / by e-mail | PDF | Already supported by its own parser (`server/importers/rewe.ts`), incl. pickup notices and reconciliation. |
| EDEKA | eBon via QR code on the checkout display, or automatically by e-mail for EDEKA app users | PDF (viewable 24 h after scanning, downloadable) | Rolled out first in EDEKA Nord (also Marktkauf). Merchant-run stores print the merchant's name in the header ("EDEKA Center … e.K."). Loyalty: DeutschlandCard. |
| Netto Marken-Discount | "digitaler Kassenbon" in the Netto app (switch in the app settings) | in-app; no documented PDF export | PAYBACK is linked to the app. Quantity lines are printed *before* the item. |
| Kaufland | "Papierbon sparen" switch in the Kaufland Card (app) | in-app, receipts kept 3 years, **can be saved/shared as PDF** | Kaufland Card discounts appear as separate minus lines. |
| Lidl | e-Bon in Lidl Plus ("Spare dir den Papier-Bon") | in-app (the unofficial `lidl-plus` API returns JSON with `quantity`, `currentUnitPrice`, `isWeight`, `discounts[]`, `deposit`) | Digital deposit receipt ("digitaler Pfandbon") nationwide since 4 Aug 2025: the deposit is credited in the app and redeemed at checkout → a "Pfandrückgabe" minus line. |
| Aldi Süd / Nord | paper; no app receipt found | printed / photographed | Article numbers before the name, quantity lines below, "Handeingabe" for hand-weighed goods. Legal names "ALDI SE & Co. KG" (Süd) and "ALDI GmbH & Co. KG" (Nord, regional companies). |
| Penny | paper; the Penny app has its own bonus programme since leaving PAYBACK (instant discounts, "Rabattsammler") | printed | REWE group layout (like REWE eBons); the bonus discount is a minus line on the whole purchase. |
| dm | eBon in the dm app | in-app | Numeric tax codes (1 = 19 %, 2 = 7 %), PAYBACK points line. |
| Rossmann | digital receipt in the Rossmann app (customer account, scan the app at checkout; technology by anybill) | in-app | Usable for returns. Coupons as minus lines. |

**Consequence for ToHuus Hub:** only REWE, EDEKA (QR/e-mail) and Kaufland (share as PDF) reliably give a
household a PDF. For everything else the realistic paths are a photo (vision model or MCP assistant), pasted
text, or the receipt e-mail inbox. That is why the generic parser accepts `pdftotext -layout` output, plain
text and HTML mail bodies alike, and why the anonymised fixture can also be rebuilt from recognised lines when
no receipt text exists (photos).

## What every German till receipt has (and what must never leave the household)

Since 2020 receipts must carry (§ 6 KassenSichV): name and address of the business, date with start and end
time of the transaction, quantity and type of goods, transaction number, amounts with VAT rate and VAT amount,
amount per payment method, serial numbers of the till and of the TSE security module, the check value
(signature) and the signature counter. Electronic receipts are allowed (JPG, PNG, PDF).

So a real receipt carries, besides the purchase itself: the **store address** (= where the household shops,
often close to home), **exact time**, **TSE transaction/serial/signature data** (uniquely identifies the
purchase), **card data** (masked PAN, terminal, trace and authorisation numbers), **loyalty data** (PAYBACK /
DeutschlandCard / Lidl Plus / Kaufland Card numbers, points and balances), sometimes **names** (cashier "Es
bediente Sie …", customer on order documents, greetings in apps) and **links with personal tokens**. REWE
pickup notices add customer number, e-mail address and order number. All of these are handled by
`server/lib/redact.ts` (see below).

## Line structures the generic parser now understands

All synthetic fixtures add up to their printed total after parsing (tested in `test/quality.test.ts`).

| Structure | Example | Handling |
|---|---|---|
| Item with tax code | `Hafermilch Barista 1l   1,79 A` (dm: `… 0,95  1`) | item; tax code → VAT rate from the tax table |
| Quantity below the item | `  2 Stk x 1,29` / `  3 x 0,99` | qty and unit price of the item above |
| Quantity before the item (Netto) | `  2 x 0,89` then `Gurke Bio  1,78 A` | applied to the next item when the product matches |
| Name line + quantity line with total (Kaufland) | `K-Classic Butter 250g` / `3 x 1,99   5,97 A` | one item |
| Inline quantity (Lidl) | `Skyr Natur 450g   1,19 x 3   3,57 A` | qty 3 |
| Quantity glued to the name | `Pfand 6 x 0,25   1,50 B` | name "Pfand", qty 6 |
| Weighed goods | `0,548 kg x 2,99 EUR/kg`, `1,234 kg * 1,99 €/kg`, `Handeingabe 0,486 kg x 2,99 EUR/kg`, also before the item | weight on the item (note "0,486 kg") |
| Deposit / empties | `Pfand 0,25`, `PFAND 0,25 … *`, `Leergut -2,25`, `Pfandrückgabe -0,75` (Lidl digital Pfandbon) | status `deposit`, never booked into stock |
| Item discount | `Preisvorteil -0,60`, `Mengenrabatt -0,30`, `Aktion 3 für 2 -1,99`, `K-Card Rabatt -0,50` | folded into the item above |
| Discount naming another item | `Rabatt Babylove 10% -1,30` | folded into the item it names, not the one above |
| Discount with another tax code | coupon `A` after a `B` item | nearest item with that tax code |
| Discount on the whole purchase | `Rabattsammler 5% auf den Einkauf -0,36`, coupons after the deposit block (`Lidl Plus Coupon … -0,28`) | `meta.receiptDiscount`, included in the sum check |
| Cashier void | `Storno Küchenrolle … -2,49`, Aldi: same item with minus price + `Storno` line | the item is removed again (or one piece of a multi-quantity line) |
| Return | `Rückgabe Duschgel Sensitive -1,25` + `-1 x 1,25` | own line, status `returned`, qty -1, skipped by default |
| Store detection | header name, then web address / company name further down (`Lidl Vertriebs-GmbH`, `ALDI SE`, `Netto Marken-Discount Stiftung`, `Kaufland Card`) | `meta.storeVia` = `header` / `text`; a store the household corrected once is recognised by its **VAT id** (or first header line) from then on (`storeVia` = `learned`) |

Not attempted on purpose: matching chain VAT ids from a built-in list (they are per company/region and change;
a wrong list would be worse than none). Instead the household teaches the VAT id once.

## Anonymised fixtures ("Help improve recognition")

`server/lib/redact.ts` keeps the structure and scrubs values: chain and legal-form words stay, the merchant's
name becomes "Muster", address → "Musterstraße 1 / 12345 Musterstadt", phone → "01234 567890", VAT/tax numbers,
card/terminal/trace numbers, loyalty numbers and balances → zeros, names → "Muster", e-mails → name@example.org,
links → host only (known chain domains) or example.org, TSE values → `[removed]`, dates → 1st of the month,
times → full hour, and any remaining run of 5+ digits outside item lines → zeros. Item lines keep names and
prices (only 8+ digit codes such as voucher numbers are masked). Two-column layouts (REWE notices) are
scrubbed column by column. The fixture reports whether the anonymised text still parses the same way.
Nothing is sent: the person sees a preview, confirms it, and downloads it or opens a prefilled GitHub issue
or e-mail themselves.
