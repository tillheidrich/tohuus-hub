# Teilen: Listen, Ideen und Karten

Decision of 15 Oct 2026. Till: "Listen, Ideen usw. auch extern an Apple Notes oder per Mail verschicken – und so erfahren Leute, dass man sein Leben damit organisiert. Fakten und Rückblicke als Share-Grafiken in 4:5 und 9:16 mit dem richtigen Branding."

Research: `docs/research/2026-10-15-apple-ai-viral.md`. That page covers the case studies (Spotify Wrapped, Strava, Duolingo, Tally badge, Hotmail footer), the specs and the export paths.

## 1. Text to Notes, Mail and Messages

Everything that is a list can leave the app as plain text:
- the shopping list (already there);
- "Im Kopf" (one entry or all of mine);
- tasks that are due;
- the week plan;
- a wish list (already there).

- **iOS:** `ShareLink(item: text)` opens the share sheet. Notes, Mail, Messages, WhatsApp and Reminders are all in it. Notes receives plain text, not a checklist (see the research).
- **Web:**
  - `navigator.share({ title, text })` where available.
  - Otherwise a small menu with "E-Mail" (`mailto:` with subject and body, the body capped at about 1,800 characters because Outlook cuts longer ones), "Kopieren" and "Als Datei (.txt)".
- **Format:** a title line, then one line per item ("– Milch", "☐ Fenster putzen", "✓ Kinderarzt angerufen").

### The footer (viral loop 1)

The last line of every shared text is:

> — geteilt aus ToHuus · tohuus.heidrich-digital.de

- `site_url` comes from `/api/me`.
- The link carries `?utm_source=share&utm_medium=<list|mind|tasks|plan|card>&utm_campaign=app`. Values are lowercase and never contain a user or household id.
- **Switching it off:** a household admin can turn it off (`PUT /api/share/settings { footer: false }`; `/api/me` shows `share_footer`). It is on by default, like Tally's "Made with" badge, which brings about 10 % of Tally's sign-ups. It is one line, and nothing is ever sent without the person tapping Share themselves.

## 2. Share cards (images)

These are cards for Instagram, WhatsApp Status or a family group. The person creates them on purpose with "Als Bild teilen".

| Format | Size | Safe zone |
|---|---|---|
| Feed 4:5 | 1080 × 1350 | 72 px margin all round |
| Story / Status 9:16 | 1080 × 1920 | no text in the top 260 px or the bottom 360 px (the platforms' UI sits there) |

### Design (the same on web canvas, iOS `ImageRenderer` and the website script)

- **Background by area:**
  - tasks, achievements and the default: green `#176b50` with text `#fffdf9`;
  - cooking: clay `#c56b4b` with text `#fffdf9`;
  - money: blue `#2f5e7a` with text `#fffdf9`;
  - pantry: mustard `#e8b865` with ink `#181d1a`;
  - facts: canvas `#f7f5ef` with ink `#181d1a` and a green accent.
- **Decoration:** a big arch in the shape of the house door from the brand mark, drawn in the text colour at 8 % opacity at the bottom right and partly cut off. Nothing else.
- **Top left:** the brand mark (house with a door, 64 px) and "ToHuus" in Hedvig Letters Sans 46 px.
- **Eyebrow:** for example "UNSERE WOCHE · 6.–12. OKT.", in Familjen Grotesk 600, 30 px, uppercase, letter spacing 0.08 em, 80 % opacity.
- **Big value:** Hedvig Letters Serif, 300 px on the story and 240 px on the feed. Only one number or one word per card.
- **Title:** Familjen Grotesk 600, 60 px, at most 3 lines.
- **Text:** 40 px at 85 % opacity. A list of up to 3 items, 38 px, each with "·".
- **Bottom line:** "Gemacht mit ToHuus · tohuus.heidrich-digital.de", 28 px at 70 % opacity. It is always there, because the card is branded.
- **Facts:** the bottom line becomes "Quelle: <source, year>". A fact without a source is never published.
- **Not used:** emojis, gradients and stock photos.

### What is on a card – privacy

- **Household cards** ("Das habt ihr geschafft"): no names, no household name, no money amounts.
- **Personal cards** ("Das hast du geschafft"): no name. Only the person themselves can make them.
- **Never on a card:**
  - private entries (Im Kopf, private tasks, personal money);
  - anything about someone a member looks after (`carefor`, health data);
  - absences with a reason.
- **"Für jemanden da gewesen":** it can be shared, but the card text is neutral ("3 Mal für jemanden da gewesen"), without the person's name.

### Where to find it

- **Wochen- und Monatsrückblick:** every card has "Teilen" with a choice of 4:5 or 9:16, and so does the whole week as a summary card.
- **Jahresrückblick:** only when the server enables it.
- **Ratgeber facts:** the website and the in-app Ratgeber cards. The images are generated by `scripts/share-cards.mjs` into `site/assets/share/`.

## Text fills the card (15 Oct 2026)

Till: "Der Text auf den Grafiken für Social soll das Bild besser ausfüllen." The rule is the same for all three renderers: web `layoutCard` in web/src/share/cards.tsx, iOS `ShareCardLayout` in ShareCardViews.swift, and the website in scripts/share-cards.mjs.

- **One scale k** (0.7–2.4) for value, title, text and list. The renderer takes the largest k at which the content fits between the brand line and the footer, and centres it in that space.
  - Story: 40 px extra at the top and 96 px above the footer.
  - Feed: 8 px and 64 px.
- **Sizes at k = 1:**

  | Element | Size | Limits |
  |---|---|---|
  | Eyebrow | 30, never scales (it is a label) | – |
  | Value, up to 4 characters | story 360 / feed 300 | one line, at most 720 / 560 |
  | Value, longer | story 280 / feed 230 | one line, at most 720 / 560 |
  | Title | 60 | at most 124, ≤ 4 lines |
  | Text | 40 | at most 66, ≤ 10 lines, so a sourced fact is never cut |
  | List | 40 | at most 68, ≤ 6 items without text, otherwise 3 |

- **One loud element per card:** the number. Typography carries the card, not decoration.
- **No opacity on text:** eyebrow, text and footer use the area's accent colour (contrast ≥ 4.5:1). Only the decorative arch keeps 8 % opacity.
