Mental load

The questions one person usually carries around.

In many families one person knows what is missing, what needs eating and where the parcel tape lives. ToHuus Hub takes that part over, so you can share it, or stop thinking about it altogether.

  • Do we still have milk?
  • What are we eating tonight?
  • Who is getting that?
  • Where is the parcel tape?
  • Is the yoghurt still good?
  • Did you already buy it?
  • What do we need for Saturday?
  • When was the water filter due?
1Everyday

One look in the morning is enough.

“Today” shows on one page what needs eating, what has run out and what is for dinner. The shopping list is current on every phone at once.

  • TodayEat first, run out, running low, tonight. That is all it shows, and on most days that is all you need.
  • Shopping list with “I’ll get it”Whoever takes something taps it, so nobody buys it twice. In the shop the list sorts itself by aisle, even without signal.
  • Discreet itemsNot everything is for everyone. Private items only show up for you, so Saturday’s present stays a surprise.
  • Ticked means put awayWhat you tick off in the shop goes straight into stock.

2Cooking

What are we eating tonight? Decided in a minute.

ToHuus Hub suggests what fits what you have and plans the week with you. Anyone who wants to can vote.

  • What works right now?What you can cook now, what lacks just one thing and what needs using up. Or one tap on “You decide for us”.
  • Week planFree evenings fill up with fitting recipes, rotating over several weeks if you like. Whatever the week is missing goes on the list in one tap.
  • Vote togetherEveryone swipes through suggestions on their own. You cook what several people like. No ranking, no debate at the stove.
  • Servings for one eveningGuests on Friday? The amount changes for that evening only, the recipe stays as it is.

3Recipes

Your version, not the internet’s.

Recipes come from wherever you already keep them. Then you cook them the way your household does.

  • Import from almost anywhereRecipe sites, open recipe databases, other recipe apps, a photo or plain text. English recipes work too, and cups and ounces turn into grams if you cook metric.
  • Cookidoo and ThermomixYour Cookidoo week comes in through Home Assistant. Thermomix steps keep time, temperature and speed.
  • The house versionRice instead of potatoes, no coriander, oat milk instead of milk. Set it once and it applies to suggestions, cooking and the shopping list.
  • Who liked it?Everyone can rate recipes and keep favourites. That makes for better suggestions, not a competition.

4Stock

Stock without a spreadsheet.

Nobody has to count pasta. Start with what runs out and leave the rest.

  • “Only what runs out”A start mode for anyone who does not want an inventory: ToHuus Hub only keeps track of what needs buying again.
  • Half full is an answerFruit and bread are full, half, low or empty. Packs get counted, coffee from the machine not at all.
  • Labels for leftovers and the freezerLeftovers and frozen food get a date and a QR label with their compartment. Scan, one tap, and it is out of stock.
  • Where is what?Every item can have an exact spot. Type “plasters” and you know which shelf.

5Receipts & spending

The receipt puts things away.

Digital receipts and orders bring your stock up to date. Typing is the fallback, not the plan.

  • Receipts from many shopsGerman eBons and pickup notes, receipts from Lidl, Aldi, dm and others, plus UK, US and Swedish formats. As PDF, photo, QR code or through your own receipt inbox.
  • Learns from correctionsFix once what a receipt abbreviation stands for, and ToHuus Hub recognises the item by itself next time.
  • Check, don’t guessEvery import goes through a short review: substitutions, missing items, new products.
  • Spending without a ledgerMonthly totals by shop and category, straight from the receipts. And a hint when something got more expensive.

6Family & guests

For everyone at home. Including the ones just visiting.

Everyone has their face in the app. Kids need no account, guests only see what you share.

  • Faces, not name listsA photo or an animal avatar, so even kids can see who is getting what.
  • Kids without a loginA tablet becomes a kids’ screen: wish for something, look up where things are. No password, a parent PIN to leave.
  • Permanent guests with levelsGrandparents or flatmates see as much as you want: ordering only, stock, recipes or everything. Switch it for a weekend if you need to.
  • Babysitter link with voice commandsA link shows where things are, with your note on top. With Home Assistant the sitter can also use voice for the lights and so on.
  • Party and order modeGuests scan a QR code and order from what is actually there. The TV shows what is ready. On normal days, that is how someone orders a coffee from the sofa.

7Away

Holidays without forgotten yoghurt.

Tell ToHuus Hub when you are away. It plans the rest around the trip.

  • Use it up beforeIn the days before, suggestions empty the fridge. Nothing fresh goes on the list.
  • Quiet while awayWhile you are gone, only urgent notifications come through.
  • Coming homeMilk, bread and what tomorrow’s breakfast needs: the welcome list is ready before you unlock the door.

8Smart home

Home Assistant, both ways.

If you already have automations, ToHuus Hub joins in instead of replacing them.

  • Installed via HACSA native integration with ready-made dashboards.
  • Two directionsShopping list as a to-do list for Assist, meal plan and home care as calendars, sensors for tonight, low stock and what is due. And what you already automate in Home Assistant reports back into the app.
  • Kitchen displayAn old tablet or the TV shows the day. Dimmed at night, dark mode included.

Privacy & self-hosting

It stays at home.

One Docker container, one SQLite file. No account with us, no tracking, nothing phones home. Everything that talks to the outside world is optional.

Runs almost anywhereDocker on a NAS or a small VPS.
Your dataOne SQLite file, JSON export, nightly backups, optionally to WebDAV or Nextcloud.
Sign-inPasskeys, e-mail link, OpenID Connect, PIN on the family tablet.
Reading photosOptional, with a model of your choice. Locally too, for example with Ollama.
LanguagesEnglish, German and Swedish. Each new one is a single file.
Open sourceAGPL-3.0. No company behind it, no subscription.

Install

  1. Save the compose fileFill in BASE_URL, your name and a password.
  2. Start itdocker compose up -d, then open http://localhost:8080.
  3. Empty or demoOn first start you choose to begin empty or to look around first.
  4. Put HTTPS in frontYour usual reverse proxy is enough. Then add the app to your home screen.
docker-compose.yml
services:
  tohuus:
    image: ghcr.io/tillheidrich/tohuus-hub:latest
    restart: unless-stopped
    ports:
      - "8080:8080"
    volumes:
      - ./data:/data
    environment:
      BASE_URL: https://home.example.org
      ADMIN_NAME: Alex
      ADMIN_PASSWORD: change-me
      LANGUAGE: en # or de, sv
To HuusLow German for “at home”. Spoken along the North Sea and the Baltic.

ToHuus Hub was built for an ordinary family life: a fridge, a freezer compartment, a pantry shelf that is never quite tidy and a cupboard where things like to disappear.

For years one person knew where everything was and what was running out, and the other one asked. The one rule while building it: it has to work on a tired Tuesday.

It is free and licensed under the AGPL. Issues and pull requests are welcome, and so are translations.
