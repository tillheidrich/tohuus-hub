Now in ToHuus

Shopping, cooking and money belong together.

What you buy is what you cook, and that is where the money goes. So ToHuus looks after all three. And after the rest of what makes a home: the tasks that keep coming back, doing each other a good turn and keeping track of wishes.

Shopping

ToHuus remembers what is missing.

From your receipts and what you tick off the list, ToHuus learns how often you buy things. When something is due again, it shows up as a suggestion with its reason. You do not have to keep a stock list for that.

  • One list, live on every phone, also while someone is in the shop
  • Whoever takes something taps “I’ll get it”
  • Sorted by aisle in the shop, even without signal

Cooking

You can cook this today.

The cook planner starts with what is at home. It shows what works right now, where only one ingredient is missing and what should be used soon. Plan the week if you like, and whatever it needs goes on the list in one tap.

  • Recipes by hand, from a link, a photo or other apps
  • Many at once as a recipe pack, even built by your own AI
  • With or without a kitchen machine, and allergies are taken into account

Money

You see where the money for food and household goes.

Every receipt counts. ToHuus shows what a month cost, in which shop and for which category, and compares it with the month before. You do not need to keep a ledger.

  • Spending by month, shop and category
  • Price changes on things you buy often
  • Budgets per week, month or year, shared or just for you, with a note at 80 percent
  • CSV export

Tasks

Whose turn is it to take out the bins?

Cleaning the bathroom, changing the bed linen, the water filter or the chimney sweep’s visit: ToHuus remembers what is due when and who does it. If you take turns, “Done” passes the task on to the next person. There are no rankings, just a calm look at who keeps what in mind, and stars only if you switch them on.

  • Once, recurring or when needed, for everyone or only for you
  • Appointments with a time show up in Apple Calendar, Google or Outlook, tasks in Apple Reminders
  • Children can have a turn too, and what nobody keeps in mind gets noticed

Wishes

Wish lists that give nothing away.

Birthdays, Christmas or just a list of things to remember: wishes with a photo, several links, a price and a note. The family sees who reserved what, the person it is for never does. Send a whole list or part of it as a message.

  • Photo, links, price and “who could give this”
  • Private lists only you can see
  • Share as a link or a ready-made message

From the guest Wi-Fi to the coffee machine.

Whoever stays over, looks after the kids or feeds the cat usually asks the same things: what is the Wi-Fi password, how does the heating work, when do the kids go to bed? Write it down once in the house info, and from then on you just send a link.

  1. Guest Wi-FiNetwork and password are kept safe with the codes. Tap “Show” and a QR code appears, and your visitors join with their phone camera.
  2. Where things arePlasters, torch, fuse box, water stopcock. With “Where is what?” the babysitter finds the biscuits too, but only in the places you share.
  3. How things work hereHeating, dishwasher, blinds, coffee machine: explained in a few lines, with a photo or a short video if you like. With Alexa, Google, Siri or Home Assistant the right voice commands are listed too.
  4. House rules and kidsWhat the kids may do, when bedtime is and whatever else goes in your home, such as shoes off in the hall.
  5. In an emergencyImportant numbers come first. Emergency, poison advice, neighbours and your own numbers come as a template, and you add the GP.

Getting started

However digital your shopping already is.

If shopping is going digital anyway, you should get something out of it too. ToHuus works with what you already have. One of these is enough to start.

  1. Delivery and e-receiptsIf you order groceries online or get digital receipts, the order and the receipt come in as a PDF. ToHuus matches them and books what was actually delivered.
  2. Snap the receiptFor a paper receipt, a photo is enough. Anything ToHuus cannot read with confidence is shown to you for a check before it is booked. Text recognition runs without AI, on the server or right on your iPhone.
  3. Just say itTap the round assistant button and say: “We are out of milk, and we need something for Sunday.” The assistant puts it on the list and tells you what it added. Every step can be undone.

You decide what goes on the list.

ToHuus makes suggestions and never quietly writes on your list. Every line shows where it came from.

  1. Collect purchasesEvery receipt and every tick on the list counts as a purchase. No more upkeep than that.
  2. Spot the rhythmAfter a few weeks ToHuus sees, for example, that you buy porridge oats about every 20 days.
  3. SuggestWhen it is due again, a suggestion appears with its reason. One tap puts it on the list.
  4. Hold back“Still have it” postpones the suggestion. Turn it down twice and ToHuus stops asking.

For heads with a lot going on.

With ADHD, autism or just a packed week, starting is often the hardest part: where do I begin, what was due again, and how long will it even take? A few things in ToHuus are built for exactly that. They help everyone in the household, and nobody has to explain why they use them.

  • Small stepsEvery task can be split into small steps you tick off one by one. “Split into small steps” suggests some: with AI if your household allows it, otherwise from ready-made steps for cleaning the bathroom, the kitchen, laundry, hoovering or changing the bed. When the task is done, the steps are open again for next time.
  • One thing at a timeThe focus view shows just one thing: the next task and its next step, large and without a list around it. Below it: “Done”, “Later” and “Too big? Make it smaller”.
  • Visible timerFive minutes, just to get going. A ring shows how much time is left, and a gentle signal sounds at the end. Nothing is counted or analysed.
  • Gentle modeA setting just for you: no red overdue warnings and no counters, things that are due say “could be done”. “Fresh start” moves everything overdue to a new start, no guilt. Notifications arrive bundled in one message at a time you choose, and Today shows at most three things.
  • No rankings, no streaksThere are no streaks and no rankings. Stars only exist if you switch them on yourselves, and gentle mode hides them. “Who keeps what in mind” lists names alphabetically, and shares only if everyone in the household agrees.
  • Mental load check33 cards for the invisible work, from “Noticing when something is running out” to “Remembering birthdays”. Everyone answers privately; the household sees the answers only when everyone agrees. Each card says what ToHuus takes off your head.
  • Out of sight, still in mind“Where is what?” knows where the batteries are and what is in the freezer. If you like, keep a stock list and see what is at home and what is running out.
  • Out of your head, fastOne tap on the assistant, one sentence, and it is on the list or with the tasks before the thought is gone again.
  • Extras in the weekly shopYou set how much a week there is for extras, as an amount or a number of items. Mark items on the list as an extra, and the list shows how much of the allowance is used. Nothing is blocked, you just keep the overview, impulse buys included.

ToHuus follows “Reduce motion” on your device, and on days when getting up is hard there is “Bring me something”. ToHuus is not a health or therapy service. It just takes a few things off your mind.

Calm by design

Built so you can put it down again quickly.

We have not measured what ToHuus does to your stress or your screen time, so we do not promise anything about it. What we can promise is how ToHuus is built.

  • No feed and nothing to keep scrolling. Every page has an end.
  • No streaks, no points, no rankings. Stars only if you switch them on yourselves.
  • Notifications bundled if you like, at a time you choose. We never send promotional pushes.
  • “Done for today”: when everything important is done, Today says so. Feel free to put the phone down.
  • The evening question only appears on Today, without a push and at most every other evening. You can switch it off.
  • A lot works without opening the app: with Siri, the widget or the Apple Watch.

What we do not promise: less stress, fewer minutes on your phone or better sleep. We would have to measure that properly first, and we have not.

Privacy and security

Your receipts work for you, not for market research.

This lists only what can be checked. How the cloud is built is described openly in the source code and the documentation.

Servers in Germany
ToHuus Cloud runs on Hetzner servers in Germany.
Every household with its own key
Every household has its own encrypted database with its own key. Photos and receipt files are encrypted too.
No tracking, no ads
Your receipts are neither sold nor analysed for advertising. This website sets no cookies either.
AI only through Mistral
Receipt photos, voice input and the assistant run on Mistral, a provider based in France. Requests are routed through OpenRouter (USA), which does not store their content (details in the privacy policy). Every request is pinned to Mistral with no fallback to other providers and is not used for training. In the cloud ToHuus asks first: without your consent nothing goes to a language model. Voice recordings are not stored.
Sensitive details stay at home
Allergies and details about children only go to a language model if you explicitly switch that on.
Mails only if you want them
Switch off the summary and the subscription reminder under Settings › Notifications › Mails from ToHuus, or with one click in each of these mails. We never send advertising.
AI can be switched off
For the whole household, just for you or for a single child. Then no request goes to a language model, and the list, suggestions, cooking and money keep working. In the cloud it is off for children until a parent switches it on.
Apple Intelligence on the iPhone only
If you like, Apple Intelligence tidies up receipt photos and handwritten lists right on the iPhone. It is off on every device until you switch it on, and nothing leaves the device for it.
Text recognition without AI
Receipt photos are first read by text recognition without a language model on the server, or your iPhone reads them itself and sends only the text. Only if that does not add up and AI is on does Mistral read the photo.
Your assistants only if you want
You connect Claude or ChatGPT yourself through MCP. They see discreet items only as a placeholder, and an admin can switch MCP off.
When you delete
When you delete the household, its key is destroyed first, then the data and its backups follow. Backup copies of the account directory expire after their retention period.

Technical details about the cloud

For everyone with their own server.

One Docker container and one SQLite file, free under the AGPL-3.0. In the browser ToHuus runs as a web app you can add to your home screen. You connect the iPhone app to your server, most easily with the QR code from the web app. You do not need an account with us.

Home Assistant, both ways
The shopping list as a to-do list for Assist, sensors for what is due and the week plan as a calendar. Installed via HACS, the integration is MIT licensed.
Receipt inbox, Telegram, printer
Receipts arrive by e-mail, “2 milk, bread” in a chat lands on the list, and a receipt printer in the kitchen prints it.
AI is optional
With a key for any OpenAI-compatible service, such as Mistral, local ones included. Without a key everything keeps working: receipt photos are read by text recognition on your server, and the assistant understands the common short sentences.
Your data
One SQLite file, encrypted if you like, with nightly backups and JSON export.
AI assistants via MCP
Claude, ChatGPT and other assistants connect straight to your household through MCP.

Questions

What people often ask.

Do I have to type everything in?

No. Receipts come in as a PDF or a photo. With Plus your household gets its own receipt address in the cloud (coming soon), and self-hosted it works through your own inbox. What you tick off the list counts as a purchase too. You do not have to keep a stock list.

Does it work without online grocery delivery?

Yes. Paper receipts come in as a photo, and if you do not want to collect receipts at all, simply tick things off the list.

Is there an Android app?

There is no separate Android app. In the browser ToHuus runs as a web app you can add to your home screen, on Android just like on a computer.

Is there an iPhone app?

Yes, on the App Store, for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch. It works with ToHuus Cloud and with your own server.

Do I need AI for this?

No. The list, suggestions, cook planner and spending all work without it. Receipt photos are read first by text recognition without AI, on the server or right on the iPhone. A language model only helps when that is not enough, and with open questions to the assistant. On newer iPhones Apple Intelligence can help on the device if you like. In the cloud AI only starts with your consent, and you can switch it off for the household, for single people and for children.

Can I bring my recipes?

Yes. From a link to a recipe site, from Instagram, TikTok, Pinterest or YouTube, from a photo, from apps like Mealie, Paprika or Nextcloud Cookbook, or many at once as a recipe pack with photos. If you connected Claude or ChatGPT, your own AI can save hundreds of recipes with their photos in one go.

And Cookidoo, Monsieur Cuisine or Cookit?

Share a recipe from Cookidoo, Monsieur Cuisine, Cookit or another kitchen machine app with ToHuus or paste the link, then confirm the name and ingredients. Add the steps yourself, paste them as text or let ToHuus read them from your screenshots. ToHuus never fetches these sites itself, and what you bring along stays private in your household.

What is the house info?

Cards for everyone who looks after your home or comes to stay: the guest Wi-Fi with a QR code, how the heating and the coffee machine work, house rules, bedtimes and important numbers, with photos and short videos if you like. Babysitters open it through a link without an account, offline once opened, or you print it as an A4 sheet. For holidays there is the handover: what the house sitter should do and when, with photos.

Do guests see our codes?

Only the ones you share, until a date if you like. Every code stays hidden until someone taps “Show”, and you see who looked when. Codes never go to an AI, to Home Assistant or into notifications.

Is ToHuus good for ADHD?

ToHuus is built for every household, but some of it is made for heads with a lot going on: tasks in small steps, a view that only shows the next thing, a visible timer to get started and a gentle mode without red warnings. There are no streaks or rankings, and stars only if you switch them on. Whether it suits you is best found out in everyday life. It does not replace treatment or advice.

Do the others see that I use gentle mode?

No. Gentle mode is a personal setting and only changes what you see and when you get notifications.

Does ToHuus help with impulse buys?

You can set an allowance for extras, per week as an amount or a number of items. Mark items on the list as an extra, and the list shows how much of it is used. ToHuus blocks nothing, it just shows where you stand.

Do I need a Thermomix?

No. The cook planner shows recipes with and without a kitchen machine, and you can filter by it. With Plus there are also ToHuus recipes for Thermomix, Monsieur Cuisine and Cookit, with the settings for each step.

Who pays if there are four of us?

One subscription covers the whole household. Members, children, guests and connected households pay nothing.

What happens if I cancel?

Nothing is deleted. You can still look at everything and export your data; adding and changing things stops without a plan. Ticking off the shopping list keeps working for seven days. With the next subscription everything is back. Or you run ToHuus on your own server, where it is free.

What happens to my data when I delete it?

First the household’s key is destroyed, so the database can no longer be read. Then the household’s files and backups are deleted. Backup copies of the account directory, which still hold the encrypted key, expire after their retention period.

Is ToHuus a family calendar?

No. ToHuus looks after shopping, cooking, the money for both and the household tasks. Appointments such as the chimney sweep go into the calendar you already use, Apple Calendar, Google or Outlook. There is deliberately no calendar view of its own and no chat; stars only if you switch them on.
