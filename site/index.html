What ToHuus does

Shopping, cooking and money belong together.

What you buy is what you cook, and that is where the money goes. So ToHuus looks after all three.

1Shopping

ToHuus remembers what is missing.

From your receipts and what you tick off the list, ToHuus learns how often you buy things. When something is due again, it shows up as a suggestion with its reason. You do not have to keep a stock list for that.

  • One list, up to date on every phone at once
  • Whoever takes something taps “I’ll get it”
  • Sorted by aisle in the shop, even without signal

2Cooking

You can cook this today.

The cook planner starts with what is at home. It shows what works right now, where only one ingredient is missing and what should be used soon. Plan the week if you like, and whatever it needs goes on the list in one tap.

  • Recipes by hand, from a link or from other apps
  • With or without a Thermomix, and allergies are taken into account

3Money

You see where the money for food and household goes.

Every receipt counts. ToHuus shows what a month cost, in which shop and for which category, and compares it with the month before. You do not need to keep a ledger.

  • Spending by month, shop and category
  • Price changes on things you buy often
  • A budget, if you want one
  • CSV export

Getting started

However digital your shopping already is.

If shopping is going digital anyway, you should get something out of it too. ToHuus works with what you already have. One of these is enough to start.

  1. Delivery and e-receiptsIf you order groceries online or get digital receipts, the order and the receipt come in as a PDF. ToHuus matches them and books what was actually delivered.
  2. Snap the receiptFor a paper receipt, a photo is enough. Anything ToHuus cannot read with confidence is shown to you for a check before it is booked.
  3. Just speakTap “Speak” and say: “We are out of milk, and we need something for Sunday.” The assistant puts it on the list and tells you what it added. Every step can be undone.

How it remembers

You decide what goes on the list.

ToHuus makes suggestions and never quietly writes on your list. Every line shows where it came from.

  1. Collect purchasesEvery receipt and every tick on the list counts as a purchase. No more upkeep than that.
  2. Spot the rhythmAfter a few weeks ToHuus sees, for example, that you buy porridge oats about every 20 days.
  3. SuggestWhen it is due again, a suggestion appears with its reason. One tap puts it on the list.
  4. Hold back“Still have it” postpones the suggestion. Turn it down twice and ToHuus stops asking.

Who it is for

For every household that does a food shop.

On first start ToHuus only asks who belongs to the household. What you see follows from that.

  • Just meNothing to remember, and at the end of the month you see what food and household cost.
  • The two of usBoth of you know what is missing. Nobody has to ask whether there is coffee left.
  • FamilyEveryone writes on the same list. Children and regular guests only see what you share.
  • Flat shareShared things go on one list, and the others do not see private items.
  • Looking after someoneIf you shop for parents or grandparents too, connect the two households and see what is missing there.

ToHuus does not assume a cellar, a chest freezer or a Thermomix. You decide which places your home has.

Privacy and security

Your receipts work for you, not for market research.

This lists only what can be checked. How the cloud is built is described openly in the source code and the documentation.

Servers in Germany
ToHuus Cloud runs on Hetzner servers in Germany.
Every household with its own key
Every household has its own encrypted database with its own key. Photos and receipt files are encrypted too.
No tracking, no ads
Your receipts are neither sold nor analysed for advertising. This website sets no cookies either.
AI only through Mistral
Receipt photos, voice input and the assistant go through OpenRouter to Mistral, a provider based in France. Every request is pinned to Mistral with no fallback to other providers, and according to OpenRouter only goes to providers that do not collect user data. Voice recordings are not stored.
Sensitive details stay at home
Allergies and details about children only go to a language model if you explicitly switch that on.
When you delete
When you delete the household, its key is destroyed first, then the data and its backups follow. Backup copies of the account directory expire after their retention period.

Technical details about the cloud

Self-hosting

For everyone with their own server.

One Docker container and one SQLite file, free under the AGPL-3.0. The iPhone app connects to your server, and in the browser ToHuus runs as a web app. You do not need an account with us.

Home Assistant, both ways
The shopping list as a to-do list for Assist, sensors for what is due and the week plan as a calendar. Installed via HACS, the integration is MIT licensed.
Receipt inbox, Telegram, printer
Receipts arrive by e-mail, “2 milk, bread” in a chat lands on the list, and a receipt printer in the kitchen prints it.
AI is optional
With a key for OpenRouter or any OpenAI-compatible service, local ones included. Without a key everything works except receipt photos and the assistant.
Your data
One SQLite file, encrypted if you like, with nightly backups and JSON export.
AI assistants via MCP
Claude, ChatGPT and other assistants connect straight to your household through MCP.

Install

  1. Get it and set it upIn .env you fill in BASE_URL, your name and a password. Every other option is listed with comments in .env.example.
  2. Start itRun docker compose up -d --build, then open http://localhost:8080. Your data lives in ./data.
  3. Empty or demoOn first start you choose to begin empty or to look around with demo data first. One tap removes it later.
  4. Put HTTPS in frontYour usual reverse proxy is enough. Then connect the iPhone app to the address or add the web app to your home screen.

Why ToHuus exists

To HuusLow German for “at home”.

I built ToHuus for our own household. Only one of us knew what was still in the fridge, what we were cooking this week and whether there was coffee left, and everyone else asked. I wanted to change that without anyone having to keep a spreadsheet.

Most of the code was written by AI coding agents. My part was deciding what ToHuus should do, testing it in our everyday life and throwing out everything that does not work in a real household. Honestly, that was quite a lot.

If shopping is going digital anyway, you should get something out of it too. Now the first families are testing ToHuus, and I am curious what of it sticks in their everyday life.

Questions

What people often ask.

Do I have to type everything in?

No. Receipts come in as a PDF or a photo, and when you self-host also through your own e-mail inbox. What you tick off the list counts as a purchase too. You do not have to keep a stock list.

Does it work without online grocery delivery?

Yes. Paper receipts come in as a photo, and if you do not want to collect receipts at all, simply tick things off the list.

Is there an Android app?

There is no separate Android app. In the browser ToHuus runs as a web app you can add to your home screen, on Android just like on a computer.

When is the iPhone app coming?

Soon on TestFlight, first for a small group of test households. If you self-host, you can use the web app today.

Do I need AI for this?

No. The list, suggestions, cook planner and spending all work without it. A language model is only needed for photos of paper receipts and for the assistant.

Do I need a Thermomix?

No. The cook planner shows recipes with and without a Thermomix, and you can filter by it.

Who pays if there are four of us?

One subscription covers the whole household. Members, children, guests and connected households pay nothing.

What happens if I cancel?

The household goes back to Free. Nothing is deleted. You then see the last 60 days, and the assistant has 20 messages a month.

What happens to my data when I delete it?

First the household’s key is destroyed, so the database can no longer be read. Then the household’s files and backups are deleted. Backup copies of the account directory, which still hold the encrypted key, expire after their retention period.

Is ToHuus a family calendar?

No. ToHuus looks after shopping, cooking and the money for both. There are deliberately no appointments, chores with owners or points.
