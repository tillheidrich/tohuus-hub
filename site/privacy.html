In short

  • Your household data is stored on Hetzner servers in Germany, every household in its own encrypted database.
  • No tracking, no ads, no analytics tools. We do not sell data and do not analyse your receipts for anyone else.
  • For receipt photos, imports and the assistant we use a language model from Mistral AI (France), routed through OpenRouter (USA).
  • Allergies and details about children only go to the language model if you explicitly switch that on.
  • You can delete your household and your account in the app at any time.

3What data we process and why

Account

Your e-mail address, your name as shown in the household, your password (only as a scrypt hash, never in plain text), whether your e-mail address is confirmed, language and region. We need these to run your account, sign you in and send you the e-mails that belong to the service: address confirmation, sign-in links, password resets and household invitations. We do not send marketing e-mails.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (contract for the use of ToHuus Cloud).

Your household’s content

Everything you enter or upload in ToHuus: the shopping list with the origin of each item, stock, products, places, recipes, the week plan, receipts and order notices (as a file or photo, and the lines recognised from them with shop, date and prices), spending and budget, notes on what ToHuus remembers for you, and the people in the household with name, role, likes and dislikes. If you add children, this can include their names and birth years.

We process this data so that ToHuus works for you: the list, suggestions from your purchase rhythm, the cook planner and the spending overview. The members and guests of your household can see it within the rights you grant. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

We do not look at the content of your households. Technically, the operator of the server has access to the keys. We only use that access if you explicitly ask us to for a support request or if we are legally required to.

Allergies and intolerances (health data)

You can record allergies and intolerances for people in the household. This is health data within the meaning of Art. 9 GDPR. ToHuus only uses it within your household: to show it and to leave out unsuitable recipes when making suggestions. The legal basis is your explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR), which you give by entering it. Only enter details about other people if they agree or if you are their legal representative, for example as a parent.

Allergies and details about children only go to a language model if an admin of your household explicitly switches that on under Settings › Access & AI › Assistant & privacy. It is off by default. You can withdraw your consent at any time by deleting the details or switching it off. What happened before remains lawful.

Assistant and voice input

What you write or say to the assistant, and its answers, are stored as a conversation. Conversations belong to the person who has them; other members do not see them. They are deleted automatically 30 days after the last message, and you can delete them earlier at any time.

We do not store voice recordings. On the iPhone, speech is turned into text on the device where possible. If your iPhone does not support that for your language, iOS uses Apple’s speech recognition, which is subject to Apple’s terms (iOS asks for your permission first). If no speech recognition is available, and always in the browser, the app sends the recording to our server. There it is passed in memory to the language model to be turned into text and then discarded.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

Plan, allowances and purchases

For each household we store the plan, the end of the trial, counters for the allowances (assistant messages, AI reads for receipts and imports, uploads) and the storage used. If you buy a subscription, we store the details from the transaction signed by Apple (see Purchases through Apple). Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

Security and abuse prevention

With every sign-in we store the IP address and the browser or app identifier (user agent) together with the session, plus when it was last used. This lets you see and sign out your devices, and lets us detect abuse. For the iPhone app we store the device name with the device token and when it was last used. To limit sign-in attempts, sign-ups and outgoing e-mails, we count IP addresses and e-mail addresses briefly in the server’s memory, for one hour at most. Server error messages are written to a log.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest is running the service securely and protecting your accounts.

When you write to us

If you e-mail us, we process your address and your message to reply. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR if it concerns your account or household, otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

4Language models: Mistral through OpenRouter

Some features use a language model. For these, ToHuus Cloud sends the necessary data to OpenRouter, Inc. (USA), an intermediary for language models. OpenRouter forwards the request to Mistral AI SAS (Paris, France), which runs the model and produces the answer. Every request is pinned to Mistral with no fallback to other providers, and with the requirement that it may only go to providers that, as classified by OpenRouter, do not collect user data, meaning they do not store requests permanently and do not use them for training.

When this happens, only when you use one of these features:

  • Photos and screenshots of receipts, and receipt texts that ToHuus cannot read without a language model. Card numbers, loyalty cards, TSE data and addresses are removed from receipt texts first. Photos are scaled down and sent as they are.
  • Imports of recipes and products from links, texts, photos and screenshots, for example a photo of a paper shopping list.
  • The assistant, and voice input when the speech was not recognised on the device.

AI can be switched off: for the whole household, for each person and for each child (the parents decide). Then no request goes to a language model; receipts are read on our server with text recognition.

What the assistant is told: your household’s name, the date, who is writing, the people with their likes and dislikes, the open list without private items, what is due by your rhythm, the week plan, this week’s and this month’s spending with the budget, and what ToHuus remembers, without entries recognised as sensitive. Allergies, children’s birth years and sensitive entries only with the consent described above.

What OpenRouter stores: according to OpenRouter, it does not store the content of requests and answers unless logging is switched on in the account. We have not switched it on. OpenRouter stores metadata such as time, model and number of tokens and, according to OpenRouter, may analyse a small share of requests anonymously to assign them to categories.

We do not use your data to train models. The legal basis for the transfer is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR because you use the feature, and Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR for health data. If you want to do without a language model, do not use these features: the list, suggestions, cook planner, spending and PDF receipts from most shops work without one.

6Transfers outside the EU

We store your households’ content in Germany only. Data reaches a country outside the EU and the EEA in these cases:

  • OpenRouter (USA), when you use a feature with a language model. The basis is the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses (Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR) or, where available, a certification under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (Art. 45 GDPR).
  • GitHub (USA) when you visit this website, on the same bases.
  • Apple processes purchases under its own terms, including in the USA.

7Location and encryption

  • The ToHuus Cloud servers are hosted by Hetzner in Germany.
  • Every household has its own database, encrypted with its own random key. Photos and receipt files are encrypted one by one with AES-256-GCM, and so are the nightly backup copies.
  • The account directory (accounts, memberships, sessions, subscriptions) is encrypted too. The households’ keys are stored there only in encrypted form, under a master key that is not kept on the data storage.
  • The connection between the app, the browser and the server is encrypted with TLS.
  • Households are kept apart: every request runs in the context of exactly one household, and without that context the server refuses access. How this works is open in the source code.

8How long we keep data

A household’s content
Until the household is deleted. On deletion the household’s key is destroyed first, so its database, files and backup copies can no longer be read. Then we delete the household’s folder and its backup copies, including those in the external backup storage.
Account
Until you delete it. It disappears from the backup copies of the account directory as they expire: locally after five weeks at most, in the external backup storage after 60 days at most.
Assistant conversations
30 days after the last message, or earlier if you delete them.
Voice recordings
Not stored.
Sessions with IP address and user agent
Until you sign out, remove the device or the session expires after 180 days.
Counters for sign-in attempts
In memory, one hour at most.
Purchase records from Apple
Until the household is deleted, then we delete them too. Apple is the seller and keeps the purchase records. They disappear from the backups of the account database as those expire.
After a subscription ends
Nothing is deleted. The household goes back to the Free plan.

9Purchases through Apple

You buy ToHuus Cloud subscriptions in the iPhone app through the App Store. Apple handles payment and billing. We do not receive your payment details or your Apple ID.

Apple sends us a signed transaction and notifications about renewals, cancellations and refunds. From these we store: transaction number, product, purchase and expiry date, whether it was a test or a real purchase, and an identifier that links the purchase to your household. We verify the signature on our own server, without further service providers. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

Apple’s privacy policy applies to Apple’s processing.

10What is stored on your device

ToHuus contains no tracking, no ads, no analytics tools and no third-party components. The iPhone app only uses features of iOS itself.

  • Web app: a technically necessary session cookie that keeps you signed in (up to 180 days), and a cache so the app loads quickly and the list works without a connection. We set no other cookies.
  • iPhone app: the device token for signing in, in the iOS keychain, plus a cache for the list and for changes still waiting to go to the server. The widget and the share extension read this data on the device.
  • Permissions: camera (barcodes, receipts, the QR code of your own server), microphone and speech recognition (voice input for the assistant), adding to the photo library (saving images). The app only asks when you use the feature, and you can withdraw permission in the iOS settings.

The legal basis for storing on your device is section 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG (German Telecommunications Digital Services Data Protection Act), because it is strictly necessary for the service you asked for.

11This website

This website sets no cookies, does not track you and loads nothing from third parties. Fonts and images are served from the same server.

It is served through GitHub Pages by GitHub, Inc. (USA). When you visit it, GitHub processes technically necessary data such as your IP address to deliver the page and protect it from attacks. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest is delivering the website securely and reliably.

If you choose the dark theme, your browser remembers that choice in its local storage. It is not sent to us.

12Self-hosted servers

ToHuus is open source and can run on your own server. Whoever runs such a server is responsible for the data on it, not us. This also applies when you connect the iPhone app to your own server: the app then sends no data to us. The operator decides which AI service is used there. There are no purchases with your own server.

Independently of this, Apple processes data under its own terms when you download the app from the App Store.

13Your rights

You have the right

  • of access to the data we process about you (Art. 15 GDPR),
  • to rectification of inaccurate data (Art. 16 GDPR),
  • to erasure (Art. 17 GDPR),
  • to restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR),
  • to data portability (Art. 20 GDPR): admins of a household can export the household’s data as JSON at any time,
  • to withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future (Art. 7(3) GDPR).

Right to object (Art. 21 GDPR): where we process data on the basis of a legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), you can object at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation.

Deleting your account and household: you can do this yourself at any time in the app, in your account settings. If you are the only person in a household, it is deleted too. In shared households you leave, and the data stays for the other members.

For anything else, an e-mail to mail@tillheidrich.de is enough.

Complaints: you can lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR). The authority responsible for us is the Data Protection Commissioner of Lower Saxony (Der Landesbeauftragte für den Datenschutz Niedersachsen), Prinzenstraße 5, 30159 Hannover, Germany.

14Required data and automated decisions

For an account in ToHuus Cloud we need your e-mail address and a password, or sign-in by e-mail link. Without them we cannot create an account. Everything else is voluntary.

There is no automated decision within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR that has legal effect on you. Suggestions from ToHuus are suggestions: you decide what goes on the list.

15Changes

If ToHuus changes in a way that requires this policy to change, we will update it. We tell signed-in users about significant changes beforehand by e-mail or in the app. The version published on this page applies.
